Private by design

Your communications are yours.

  • No outside AI provider reads your messages. Prioritising and summaries use fixed rules inside the product.
  • Your data is private to you, the owner of the workspace.
  • Drafts are approval-only. Nothing is sent, forwarded or deleted without your say-so.
  • You can disconnect an account whenever you choose.

Honest answers

Is there AI chat?

Not yet. The assistant in the executive preview works from rules and your own data. It is not an open-ended AI chatbot.

Does it work with Outlook?

Not yet. Gmail and calendar are supported today.

Does WhatsApp appear inside the dashboard?

No. For now, buttons open the chat in WhatsApp Web. Reading WhatsApp inside the dashboard is not available.

Will it send messages for me?

Never automatically. Drafts wait for your approval.
